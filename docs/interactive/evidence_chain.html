The MIBiG rows are what the MIBiG adapter writes from the public entry BGC0000433 on its own, which MIBiG grades "questionable" (hence confidence 0.50). Other surfactin entries in the full release can add their record numbers and citations to the same arrows. ENCODES and CATALYZES come from the entry's gene record for srfAA (Phase 3): a knock-out showed that srfAA builds the molecule's backbone, so its CATALYZES arrow is curated_experimental, while ENCODES only states that the gene is in the cluster (from Phase 3b, MIBiG's protein file lists it too, with the cluster's 22 other proteins). UniProt's ID mapping says MIBiG's protein number CAE02630.1 is the entry Q70KJ7, Surfactin synthetase A, and since Phase 4 the two records are one node, uniprot:Q70KJ7, still named srfAA. The protein route sits beside the PRODUCES shortcut, so hiding it never breaks the chain. The two curation rows come from one row of the cited bridge (Phase 2): Xiao et al. (2023) found that purified surfactin inhibited Botrytis cinerea and reduced grey mould on harvested winter jujube. The bridge refuses any row without a citation.
